About

My name is Mariah, and one of my favorite hobbies is traveling. I have valued this hobby for some time now, as I blame my parents for putting me in a car and driving all the way down to Florida. I’ve enjoyed simple road trips from New Jersey and back to bigger trips to fly to Italy for the summer. Am I classified as a travel blogger? Not yet, as I know I haven’t traveled even a quarter of the world, but I love it so, let me take you on my journey.
