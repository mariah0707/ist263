Tips on trips

  1. Klarna is your best friend. Klarna and Affirm help you pay for a lot of things that you don’t want to put on your debit card. Klarna and Affirm are bought now and pay later applications.
  2. Try to pack as light as possible. Packing light will help you avoid unnecessary fees, especially when traveling overseas. Most of the time, toiletries are provided by Airbnbs or hotels, so you can get 3 oz bottles of toiletries to bring with you.
  3. Get an extra carry-on just in case your luggage is lost.
  4. Research. Research. Research. Social media has a lot of recommendations for the areas you are going to travel to. This helps with picking out meals and finding out hidden places in your city.
  5. Invest in Google Photos or a digital camera. There is limited storage on these phones, and you’ll be taking hundreds of photos if you are anything like me.
